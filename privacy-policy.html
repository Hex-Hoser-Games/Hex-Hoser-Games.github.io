Privacy policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

The short version

We collect as little data as possible. We don’t sell your personal information. If you email us, we use your message only to reply and provide support.

1. About this policy

This policy explains how Hex & Hoser Games (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use our games, visit this website, or contact us. It currently applies to ghIq’tal and this website.

2. Information our games collect

Our games do not directly ask for or collect personally identifiable information unless we clearly tell you at the point of collection. A game may use services provided by your device platform or storefront. Those services may process technical information such as device type, operating-system version, crash reports, and anonymous usage diagnostics under their own privacy policies.

3. Website information

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address for security and service operation. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or our own analytics on this site.

4. Information you send us

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to provide, including your email address, message, and any attachments. We use it to respond, troubleshoot, and improve our games. Please don’t send sensitive personal information.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may share information only with service providers needed to operate our games and communications, when required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our players, studio, or others.

6. Retention and security

We keep support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to resolve requests, maintain support history, meet legal obligations, and improve our products. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

8. Your choices

You may contact us to ask what personal information you have provided to us, or to request its correction or deletion. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate operational purposes.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when our games, services, or legal obligations change. We will post the new version here and revise the “Last updated” date above.

Star Trek, Klingon, and all related marks, characters, and elements belong to their respective owners. No ownership is claimed; any use is intended as fair use where applicable.

11. Contact

Questions or requests can be sent to hexhosergames@gmail.com.
